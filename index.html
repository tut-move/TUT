WHY TUT MOVE?

Capacity exists. Demand exists.
The connection is missing.

At the same moment a business is searching for a driver, a driver may have available time. A load may be waiting for a truck while a nearby truck has unused capacity. Warehouse space can sit empty while another business is searching for storage.

The problem is not always that the resource does not exist. Sometimes the two sides simply cannot find each other.

01

One market. Multiple sides.

TUT Move does not own the trucks, employ the drivers, own the loads or control warehouse capacity. It brings independent supply and demand into one marketplace.

02

AVAILABLE / WANTED

Users publish what is available or exactly what they are looking for. TUT Move helps surface relevant connections across drivers, trucks, loads and warehouse capacity.

03

The market decides.

TUT Move does not dictate the transport price. Users can set a price, request quotes, make offers, counter and agree on their own commercial terms.

FOUNDER

Built from a simple question.

Abdelaziz Metwaly

Founder, TUT Move

“I asked myself a simple question: how can one business be searching for a driver while somewhere else a driver is looking for work? How can a truck be empty while a load is searching for a truck? How can warehouse space sit unused while another business needs that exact capacity? The resources and the demand already exist — they are fragmented. TUT Move started with the idea of bringing them into one market.”
THE VISION

Turn unused capacity into opportunity.

TUT Move is being built as a multi-country, multi-language marketplace connecting transportation and storage capacity with demand — starting with drivers, trucks, loads and warehouses.
