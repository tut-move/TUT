ABOUT TUT MOVE

Connecting People. Creating Opportunities.

TUT MOVE is an online logistics marketplace connecting drivers, truck and trailer owners, shippers, warehouse owners and equipment providers.

One marketplace where people can find opportunities, connect directly, negotiate freely, and make their own decisions.

WHY TUT MOVE?

Capacity exists. Demand exists. The connection is missing.

Every day, trucks have available capacity, drivers have time, and warehouse space remains unused. At the same time, businesses are searching for exactly these resources.

The problem isn’t always a lack of availability. Sometimes, the right people simply can’t find each other.

TUT MOVE exists to help bridge that gap.

OUR PURPOSE

More Opportunities. More Freedom.

We believe everyone should have the opportunity to reach a wider market.

TUT MOVE brings independent supply and demand together, allowing users to discover opportunities, compare options, and negotiate directly.

We don’t set your prices or decide which deal is right for you.

Your business. Your terms. Your decision.

Cancellation and refund principles for marketplace agreements.

Before an agreement

A listing or unaccepted offer may generally be withdrawn without creating a completed service agreement, subject to any commitments the users have separately made.

After acceptance

Once users accept an agreement, cancellation terms should be agreed between those parties. They are responsible for costs, losses or cancellation charges arising from their underlying service relationship unless the platform expressly provides a specific rule for that transaction.

After payment

Where platform payment is enabled, a refund is not automatic merely because a user requests cancellation. Any refund will depend on the transaction status, the parties' agreement, available evidence, payment-provider rules and any mandatory rights that apply.

After pickup or service start

Once pickup, storage, driving or another service has started, users should not assume a full refund is available. The parties remain responsible for resolving the underlying service and any resulting charges, subject to applicable law and the dispute process.

Platform fees

Each participant pays the fixed USD platform fee shown before checkout. Once both participants have paid and the platform agreement is activated, ordinary cancellation of the underlying transport does not automatically refund these fees. Incorrect charges, overcharges, duplicate payments, failed activation, mandatory consumer rights and other legally required refunds remain eligible for review. If the second participant does not pay within the ten-minute payment window, captured platform fees are submitted for refund; bank processing time may vary.
